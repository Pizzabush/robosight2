Privacy Policy — Robosight

Last updated: 9/17/26

Robosight uses your device's camera to detect nearby objects and
describe them aloud. All processing happens on your device.

### Information we collect

None. Robosight does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information.

### Camera

The app accesses your camera to detect objects in view. Camera
images are processed on your device and are never saved, uploaded,
or shared. The app has no network functionality.

### Sensors

The app reads the device gyroscope to detect motion and avoid
scanning while the phone is moving. This data is used only in the
moment and is not stored.

### Data stored on your device

The only setting saved is your arm-length preference, used to
describe distances. It is stored locally on your device and removed
when you uninstall the app.

### Third parties

Robosight uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) for camera and
depth functionality. ARCore's data handling is governed by Google's
privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Robosight contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

### Children

Robosight does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.
